Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Introduction

ScholarWatch ("the Bot") is an open-source Discord bot that monitors Google Scholar for new publications. This Privacy Policy explains what data the Bot collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that data.

2. Data We Collect

The Bot collects and stores only the minimum data required to function:

  • Guild (server) ID — to associate configuration with your Discord server.
  • Channel IDs — to know where to send notifications.
  • Keywords — the search terms you configure for monitoring.
  • SerpApi API key — if you provide one, it is stored encrypted at rest and used solely to query Google Scholar on your behalf.
  • Configuration preferences — language, schedule, enabled sources.
  • Article metadata — titles, authors, and links of publications already notified, to avoid duplicates.

The Bot does not collect or store:

  • Message content from your server
  • User IDs, usernames, or any personal information about server members
  • Direct messages

3. How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used exclusively to provide the Bot's functionality:

  • Querying Google Scholar via SerpApi using your configured keywords
  • Posting publication notifications to your configured channels
  • Avoiding duplicate notifications

4. Third-Party Services

The Bot uses the following third-party services:

  • Discord API — to operate as a Discord bot. Subject to Discord's Privacy Policy.
  • SerpApi — to query Google Scholar. Your keywords are sent to SerpApi as search queries. Subject to SerpApi's terms.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database. If you self-host the Bot, you are responsible for securing your own database. If you use a hosted instance, the database is secured with standard practices including encrypted connections and restricted access.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as the Bot is active in your server. When the Bot is removed from a server, you may request deletion of all associated data by contacting the operator.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • View all stored data using /watch status and /keywords
  • Modify your data at any time through the Bot's commands
  • Delete your data by removing all watch channels and keywords, or by removing the Bot from your server and requesting data deletion

8. Children's Privacy

The Bot is not directed at individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
